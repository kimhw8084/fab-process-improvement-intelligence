## Known governing constraints (from this project history, not invented Rxx numbering)
1. Direct semiconductor FAB ENGINEERING relevance: Metrology, Photo, Etch, Deposition, Diffusion, Implant, CMP and closely related process engineering. Exclude finance, facilities, inventory, corporate administration and generic workflow-only tools.
2. Minimal company-side adaptation 40%; verifiable engineering ROI 25%; mature existing OSS reuse 20%; genuine LLM advantage 10%; global adoption/differentiation 5%. Noncompensatory gate failures cannot be offset by weighted scores.
3. Mature openly available, commercially usable and offline-capable OSS first. Never confuse public GitHub source with acceptable commercial licenses; review direct/transitive terms and target-platform constraints.
4. External one-way transfer package, complete offline dependencies, no runtime cloud/public network, and approved on-prem/intranet OpenCode/LLM endpoint compatibility.
5. Existing Fab Insight MCP governed/admin-approved knowledge and approximately 13,000 authorized data tables are an advantage, not blanket access. Use current authorization and genuine supported APIs; no redundant semantics authority.
6. One coherent engineer interface. Built functions must directly support real, repeatable engineering jobs and avoid duplicating existing separate sampling-prediction work.
7. Scientific evidence, provenance, uncertainty, correct joins, effective times, selection bias controls, falsification/counterevidence and independently measured performance matter. No unsupported root-cause or intervention claims.
8. Read-only manufacturing-data operations; no equipment actuation, unsupervised recipes/experiments, privilege elevation, or company-data transfer to public systems.
9. Six evidence stages: discovery; external build; internal compatibility; first valid engineering result; measured recurring value; scaled adoption/recognition. Never claim the later stages from an offline demo.
10. A solution must be externally prepared and tested as completely as is technically and legally possible before authorized company-side preflight.
