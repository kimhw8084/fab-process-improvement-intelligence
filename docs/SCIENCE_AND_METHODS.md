# Scientific algorithms and critical decision rules — FAB Process Improvement Intelligence

## Three evidence-permission modes and deterministic boundaries

**Level 1 OBSERVATIONAL:** classify factors as controllable vs measured environmental covariates and latent/unknown; validate units, factor ranges, recipe/product/layer and chamber contexts. Fit interpretable linear/quadratic models with justified interactions; use HC3 only for independent rows and cluster-robust covariance when repeated rows share a declared independent unit. If a caller selects a coarser cluster field, each declared independent unit must map to exactly one cluster; a unit split across clusters makes the sandwich independence assumption incompatible with the supplied unit declaration, so D01 withholds uncertainty. A 2,000-trial randomized paired-lot null challenge measured a 15.4% rejection rate when each lot's two observations were incorrectly assigned to separate chamber clusters (95% Wilson interval 13.9–17.0%), versus 4.8% when clustered by lot (3.9–5.8%). This synthetic challenge supports the refusal rule; it does not estimate a FAB false-positive rate. D01 now compares the caller formula with fixed linear, quadratic, pairwise-interaction and (when specified) no-fixed-effect variants; this same-data diagnostic reports coefficient sign reversals and never selects a favorable formula. A caller-supplied time/group holdout is evaluated by fitting on its actual training rows and reporting descriptive held-out bias, MAE, RMSE and a training-mean baseline. Split references are not authenticated; holdout scores do not establish future performance or causality. Non-identifiability prevents separate coefficient claims. Missing factor/metric values are not imputed or handled by complete-case assumptions: the dossier is refused because outcome-dependent selection can change the estimand. If `measurement_uncertainty` is supplied, D01 is refused because it is not propagated by this model; otherwise the report states that uncertainty was not supplied or propagated.

**Level 2 EXPERIMENT DESIGN:** require engineer-approved objective(s), domain, forbidden combinations, physical/quality/safety constraints and actual authorization *before suggesting candidate settings*. Build deterministic full-/fractional-factorial or response-surface/Latin designs with blocking and power assumptions. Sequential selection compares linear and caller-specified quadratic formulas only by grouped cross-validation inside development units, then scores each selected formula on a deterministic untouched independent-unit holdout. Optional competing metric constraints require explicit bounds and per-metric engineer RMSE thresholds; each is fit and validated on the same group split, and a candidate survives only when every model-based individual predictive interval lies within its supplied limits. This screen is not a feasibility or probability guarantee. The holdout is descriptive, is also used for a predeclared refusal threshold, and can be overfit through repeated inspection; it is not a future guarantee. Candidate settings, support, incumbent and acquisition are derived only from development units. A one-step expected-improvement proposal is restricted to local development support; the current joint convex-hull check is qualified for at most two factors, so higher-dimensional next-best selection refuses until a multivariate support method passes independent tests. A new `confirm_window_candidate` path consumes an exact D02 candidate plus an explicit engineer reference and emits a seeded randomized complete-pair plan. It uses a three-term Cornish–Fisher Student-t critical approximation on at least 11 pairs, normal power tails, and one extra pair, calibrated against a fixed SciPy noncentral-t oracle grid; this removed underpower across 54 tested MDE/alpha/power combinations but is not a universal or exact power guarantee. It consumes caller-supplied paired-difference SD, minimum effect, alpha, and power; missing source references, forbidden/out-of-bound settings, unrecognized D02 candidates, sample-size cap, or insufficient run budget refuse. This remains a proposal, not an execution command; variance inputs, normality, randomization feasibility and approvals require engineer review. Fixed synthetic benchmark against scikit-optimize GP EI refuses the multimodal cases and has low regret on accepted smooth cases, but does not establish engineering value. No automatic experiment launch.

**D02 process-window exploration:** distinguish observed, interpolated, extrapolated, unsupported and forbidden cells; fit the response only on training independent units; and compute local group-maximum absolute errors on disjoint calibration units. The displayed pointwise interval uses a finite 95% order-statistic rank only when at least 19 local calibration units are present; it makes no marginal, conditional, or simultaneous coverage claim. A separate finite-panel band scores each independent unit by its maximum absolute residual across the full exact-setting panel, then uses a Bonferroni allocation across all declared constrained metrics. Its target panel, model-selection reference, threshold-selection reference and metric family must be declared in a precommit record before calibration outcomes are inspected; the record and its content hash are caller supplied and are not authenticated by P4. Every calibration unit must cover exactly that complete panel. Under exchangeable calibration and future independent units, precommitted model/metric/panel/limits, and a matching complete panel, this split-conformal construction targets at least 95% marginal simultaneous predictive coverage for one future independent unit over the finite panel and declared metrics. It does not cover interpolation, extrapolation, conditional subgroups or production populations. Missing/mismatched plans or an unavailable finite calibration rank cause abstention from the simultaneous band while leaving any pointwise screen explicitly advisory. A 5,000-trial independent challenge on 25 equally distributed settings found 95.3% coverage for a fixed one-setting band, 86.3% after choosing the smallest apparent calibration radius among 25 settings, and 95.42% for the predeclared complete-panel max score; this isolates adaptive selection bias and does not qualify manufacturing exchangeability. An optional third independent evaluation partition reports exact-setting unit-level violations only as a descriptive diagnostic, and counts an observation only when every modeled non-explored factor matches the declared fixed setting; population-mismatched units are reported separately. When every selected candidate has exact outcomes for a common set of evaluation units, D02 also reports the fraction of those units infeasible at one or more selected cells. That set-wise number remains descriptive. Missing-outcome units are excluded and reported. Cross-classified held-out challenges show tool and period shifts separately, while an unmodeled nonlinear interaction can make a searched interpolated cell falsely feasible despite narrow pointwise calibration intervals. Candidates remain advisory and require separate engineer-reviewed confirmation.

**D03 cross-step tradeoffs:** require approved temporal links to downstream outcomes; multi-output model or observed Pareto frontier with uncertainty. If no downstream data, return scope-limited within-step tradeoff rather than speculation. For both observed and approved cross-step comparisons, declared objective bounds are applied at the whole independent-unit grain before aggregation, and per-setting excluded/eligible counts are surfaced. If exclusions occur, the result is limited scope and the family-wise envelope is withheld because outcome-dependent screening can change compared populations. Within-step family-wise comparison also requires identical independent-unit sets at every included setting; cross-step comparisons rely on the same declared population ID in the approved synthetic contract. Otherwise D03 reports a reasoned unavailable status. When those population conditions hold, D03 can report separate Bonferroni rectangles across the complete candidate-setting × objective mean family; it requires at least 11 and at most 301 independent units per setting, at most 40 means, and uses a conservative Cornish-Fisher Student-t critical approximation validated against SciPy's quantiles on the supported range. Its 500-trial independent oracle obtained 95.8% joint mean coverage on correlated-normal lots and 96.8% on heavy-tailed t(5) lots. The unadjusted marginal baseline covered 76.8% and 77.6%; the SciPy vectorized max-standardized bootstrap was narrower (88.3–88.6% of Bonferroni width) but covered only 90.4% and 93.6%, so it was rejected for product use. These results are finite simulations, not a population guarantee; the envelope assumes approximate normality of setting means and prespecified candidate/objective families. It prevents interval-separated Pareto claims when the rectangles overlap; the mean Pareto front remains descriptive. Never secretly choose engineering value weights or override a constraint.

**Level 3 INTERVENTION-VALIDATED:** evaluate documented experiments or interventions. Randomized comparison if truly randomized; panel DiD requires complete pre/post outcomes, assignment ledger, declared categorical `population_fields` with stable within-unit values and matching treatment/control counts, two preperiods for a pretrend placebo check, a differential-trend sensitivity bound with source, method, and units exactly matching the outcome, adequate independent units, no differential concurrent intervention, and an interval that remains directional after expanding by the declared bound. Missing population fields, composition shifts or incomplete categorical coverage leave the DiD result descriptive. When a third preperiod is supplied, adjacent preperiod slope changes test for curvature; incomplete coverage or a result outside the documented equivalence bound withholds the diagnostic gates. Optional negative-control outcomes require a declared metric and matching bound units, source/method and complete compatible panels. Passing these tests cannot prove parallel trends or remove unmeasured confounding. The numeric sensitivity interval is conditional on the supplied bound; P4 cannot authenticate its source or adequacy. D05 therefore never emits `causal_claim=true` for observational DiD, and `conditional_interpretation_eligible` remains false because the available adapter has no independent substantive qualification for the bound. It reports whether the numerical assumption gates passed separately and retains the descriptive estimate and counterevidence. Statistical evidence remains separate from production qualification. Record null/harmful experiments, not only wins.

**D03 identity and objective handling:** independent-unit and join IDs are opaque strings or integers with type-preserving identity; coercing both to display strings can merge distinct engineering units and alter a mean Pareto front. A missing/invalid observed-unit ID withholds the entire comparison rather than silently dropping a row. The mean front is descriptive; marginal or family-wise uncertainty status is separately exposed, and missing or incompatible objectives prevent a cross-step comparison.

**D04 confirmation variance:** the reported paired-difference SD is the planning input. D04 now refuses an unqualified point estimate and requires either a one-sided normal-theory chi-square upper confidence bound (at least 95%, with source degrees of freedom) or an engineer-approved hard bound. P4 records but does not recompute or authenticate that upper bound. Under a fixed normal historical-sample stress test, using a noisy point estimate as known SD led to underpowered plans in 41.1–55.8% of trials across 5–100 historical degrees of freedom; a caller-supplied one-sided 95% upper bound reduced the observed fraction to 3.0–5.15%, while increasing mean run pairs from about 35 to 43.6–141.2. These are simulation results under normal independent paired differences, not universal guarantees or engineering population evidence.

**D05 randomized missing outcomes:** assignment-ledger and observation unit IDs must be nonempty strings and match exactly; numeric `1` must never silently match string `"1"`. The ledger establishes assigned denominators. If any outcomes are missing, D05 withholds a point estimate and returns worst-case ITT arm/effect bounds only when finite caller bounds are supplied and every observed outcome lies inside them. Otherwise status is `INCONCLUSIVE`. Even a bounded result is not a point effect or verified engineering conclusion; P4 cannot authenticate randomization records or outcome bounds.

**OSS candidates:** statsmodels (baseline), DoWhy for explicit causal graphs/estimands, OR-Tools/Emukit/Ax/BoTorch for constrained designs only after an independent performance/complexity tournament, MAPIE/PyMC/pymoo for uncertainty and multiobjective scenarios as justified. Heavy dependencies are conditional, never mandatory merely from name recognition.


## Minimum correct implementation order
A. Validate source/identity/units/permissions BEFORE scientific analysis. B. Implement an independently understandable deterministic baseline and a fixed positive/negative oracle. C. Create an evidence envelope and show a complete user workflow. D. Add the full required scientific methods with assumption diagnostics and falsifying cases. E. Compare advanced OSS only where it can beat or materially extend that baseline; freeze test cases first. F. Run the combined product and real target-OS/offline qualification. G. Repeat audit until all gates pass.

## Mathematical and evaluation requirements
- For numeric estimates, specify the estimand, unit, effective independent sample, uncertainty method, algorithm/version and declared applicability domain.
- For classification, retrieval and anomaly cases, precommit held-out metrics, precision/recall/calibration or appropriately justified equivalents. Never fit thresholds to evaluation answers.
- Use group/time/device/wafer/lot/experiment-aware holdouts where dependencies require them; random measurement-row splits are not acceptable evidence of broader generalization.
- Distinguish data observed, data estimated and data unknown; refuse if physical constraints or study assumptions cannot be verified.
- Run property, metamorphic, adversarial and counterfactual *tests* where meaningful (e.g. changing labels must not create a spurious causal claim; adding an unapproved join must trigger refusal).
- An LLM explanation must be mechanically checked against the verified evidence object. An independent code-based oracle, not an agent's agreement, determines scientific pass/fail.

## Scientific peer review rubric
Engineer/reviewer should be able to answer: Is the population actually eligible? What was the measured unit and its grain? Which approved sources and joins were used? Could sampling or timing explain the observation? Is the statistical estimand identifiable? Which alternative explanations were tested? Which result is observed rather than predicted? What result would invalidate the conclusion? Does the method generalize to unseen wafers/lots/tools/periods? What is explicitly unknown?
