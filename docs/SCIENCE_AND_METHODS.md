# Scientific algorithms and critical decision rules — FAB Process Improvement Intelligence

## Three evidence-permission modes and deterministic boundaries

**Level 1 OBSERVATIONAL:** classify factors as controllable vs measured environmental covariates and latent/unknown; validate units, factor ranges, recipe/product/layer and chamber contexts. Fit interpretable linear/quadratic models with justified interactions; use HC3 only for independent rows and cluster-robust covariance when repeated rows share a declared independent unit. If a caller selects a coarser cluster field, each declared independent unit must map to exactly one cluster; a unit split across clusters makes the sandwich independence assumption incompatible with the supplied unit declaration, so D01 withholds uncertainty. A 2,000-trial randomized paired-lot null challenge measured a 15.4% rejection rate when each lot's two observations were incorrectly assigned to separate chamber clusters (95% Wilson interval 13.9–17.0%), versus 4.8% when clustered by lot (3.9–5.8%). This synthetic challenge supports the refusal rule; it does not estimate a FAB false-positive rate. Callers can declare known shared residual-dependence dimensions with `shared_dependence_fields`. P4 checks whether any declared group spans multiple independent units; when it does, the configured one-way/row covariance is withheld while the point estimate remains available. Fixed effects adjust group means only and do not establish residual independence. Undeclared common shocks remain undetected. A 1,200-trial randomized chamber×product null challenge with 1,800 independent lots measured a 33.5% false-positive rate (Wilson95 30.9–36.2%) for one-way lot-clustered Statsmodels OLS and 4.67% (3.61–6.01%) for its two-way chamber/product reference. P4 detected both crossed dimensions and withheld intervals in all 8 diagnostic fits. Statsmodels two-way covariance is a measured candidate only for exactly two declared dimensions; it is not integrated into the dependency-free product runtime because P4 must also reject unsupported three-or-more-way dependence and the current offline product deliberately avoids adding its NumPy/SciPy/Pandas/Patsy/formulaic dependency tree. This is a synthetic method comparison and conservative refusal rule, not a manufacturing false-positive or coverage estimate. D01 compares the caller formula with fixed linear, quadratic, pairwise-interaction and (when specified) no-fixed-effect variants; this same-data diagnostic reports coefficient sign reversals and never selects a favorable formula. A caller-supplied time/group holdout is evaluated by fitting on its actual training rows and reporting descriptive held-out bias, MAE, RMSE and a training-mean baseline. Split references are not authenticated; holdout scores do not establish future performance or causality. Non-identifiability prevents separate coefficient claims. Missing factor/metric values are not imputed or handled by complete-case assumptions: the dossier is refused because outcome-dependent selection can change the estimand. If `measurement_uncertainty` is supplied, D01 is refused because it is not propagated by this model; otherwise the report states that uncertainty was not supplied or propagated.

Cluster inference also checks observation-count concentration. It reports the inverse-Herfindahl effective cluster count from cluster row shares and withholds intervals if this conservative count falls below `minimum_clusters`; degrees of freedom are capped at its floored count minus one. This is an abstention heuristic, not a model-based effective sample size or coverage guarantee. In a fixed independent 1,200-trial null challenge with 30 balanced lots, the product retained intervals with 93.9% empirical coverage (95% Wilson interval 92.4–95.1%), matching the Statsmodels reference. With one lot contributing 50.8% of rows, the count-only Statsmodels baseline covered 67.4% (64.7–70.0%), while P4 withheld intervals in all 1,200 trials because the row-share effective count was 3.75. These synthetic results establish a failure mode and a conservative refusal behavior; they do not validate other imbalance patterns or manufacturing data.

For a binary factor that is constant within each declared cluster, D01 separately counts independent clusters in each exposure level. If either level contains fewer than 10 clusters, it retains identified point estimates but withholds model intervals and prediction uncertainty. This protects against treating a large nominal cluster count as adequate when only a few independent units support one arm. In a fixed-seed 3,000-trial randomized null challenge with 30 equal-size lots and heteroskedastic lot means, the ordinary Statsmodels CR1 interval with 3 exposed lots covered 90.37% (Wilson95 89.26–91.37%); a Welch reference on independent lot means covered 96.23% (95.49–96.86%). P4 withheld intervals in all 3,000 sparse-arm trials. At 10 exposed and 20 control lots, P4 retained intervals in all trials with 94.70% empirical coverage (93.84–95.45%), matching the Statsmodels coefficient interval endpoints within `1.1e-11`. The ten-lot threshold is a conservative abstention heuristic tested on this generator, not an inference guarantee or universal cutoff. Welch is a benchmark reference only for randomized equal-size independent lots; P4 does not assume that design for observational analyses.

For formally full-rank but poorly separated continuous factors, D01 now reports an elevated/severe warning derived from the squared pivoted-QR diagonal ratio. The warning accompanies (rather than replaces) marginal intervals, and explicitly labels the cutoff as a heuristic; it is not an exact condition number, coefficient-identifiability certificate, or causal test. A fixed-design 250-trial known-effect challenge used 120 observations and varied independent jitter in one predictor from 0.2 to 0.003. At the well-separated design the median proxy was 16.9, both coefficient sign-error fractions were 0%, and median interval widths were 0.84–0.87. At jitter 0.003 the median proxy was 102,613, sign-error fractions were 44.8% and 48.4%, median interval widths were about 61, and marginal interval coverage was 98% for both coefficients. At jitter 0.05 the proxy was 421 and the weaker factor's sign-error fraction was 16%. The 100 and 1,000 warning cutoffs are descriptive alert thresholds selected to expose this instability on the fixed challenge, not universal boundaries; the model still reports the intervals so engineers can see the loss of precision.

**Level 2 EXPERIMENT DESIGN:** require engineer-approved objective(s), domain, forbidden combinations, physical/quality/safety constraints and actual authorization *before suggesting candidate settings*. Build deterministic full-/fractional-factorial or response-surface/Latin designs with blocking and power assumptions. Sequential selection compares linear and caller-specified quadratic formulas only by grouped cross-validation inside development units, then scores each selected formula on a deterministic untouched independent-unit holdout. Optional competing metric constraints require explicit bounds and per-metric engineer RMSE thresholds; each is fit and validated on the same group split, and a candidate survives only when every model-based individual predictive interval lies within its supplied limits. This screen is not a feasibility or probability guarantee. The holdout is descriptive, is also used for a predeclared refusal threshold, and can be overfit through repeated inspection; it is not a future guarantee. Candidate settings, support, incumbent and acquisition are derived only from development units. A one-step expected-improvement proposal is restricted to local development support; the current joint convex-hull check is qualified for at most two factors, so higher-dimensional next-best selection refuses until a multivariate support method passes independent tests. A new `confirm_window_candidate` path consumes an exact D02 candidate plus an explicit engineer reference and emits a seeded randomized complete-pair plan. It uses a three-term Cornish–Fisher Student-t critical approximation on at least 11 pairs, normal power tails, and one extra pair, calibrated against a fixed SciPy noncentral-t oracle grid; this removed underpower across 54 tested MDE/alpha/power combinations but is not a universal or exact power guarantee. It consumes caller-supplied paired-difference SD, minimum effect, alpha, and power; missing source references, forbidden/out-of-bound settings, unrecognized D02 candidates, sample-size cap, or insufficient run budget refuse. This remains a proposal, not an execution command; variance inputs, normality, randomization feasibility and approvals require engineer review. Fixed synthetic benchmark against scikit-optimize GP EI refuses the multimodal cases and has low regret on accepted smooth cases, but does not establish engineering value. No automatic experiment launch.

**D02 process-window exploration:** distinguish observed, interpolated, extrapolated, unsupported and forbidden cells; reject null, blank, or composite independent-unit identifiers; fit the response only on training independent units; and compute local group-maximum absolute errors on disjoint calibration units. The displayed pointwise interval uses a finite 95% order-statistic rank only when at least 19 local calibration units are present; it makes no marginal, conditional, or simultaneous coverage claim. A separate finite-panel band scores each independent unit by its maximum absolute residual across the full exact-setting panel, then uses a Bonferroni allocation across all declared constrained metrics. Its target panel, model-selection reference, threshold-selection reference and metric family must be declared in a precommit record before calibration outcomes are inspected; the record and its content hash are caller supplied and are not authenticated by P4. Every calibration unit must cover exactly that complete panel and match the precommitted number of observations at every setting (one by default for the legacy single-row contract). A count mismatch withholds the finite-panel band; an explicitly larger count is accepted only when every calibration unit matches it. Under exchangeable calibration and future independent units, precommitted model/metric/panel/observation-count/limits, and a matching complete panel, this split-conformal construction targets at least 95% marginal simultaneous predictive coverage for one future independent unit over the finite panel and declared metrics. It does not cover interpolation, extrapolation, conditional subgroups or production populations. Missing/mismatched plans or an unavailable finite calibration rank cause abstention from the simultaneous band while leaving any pointwise screen explicitly advisory. A 5,000-trial independent challenge on 25 equally distributed settings found 95.3% coverage for a fixed one-setting band, 86.3% after choosing the smallest apparent calibration radius among 25 settings, and 95.42% for the predeclared complete-panel max score; this isolates adaptive selection bias and does not qualify manufacturing exchangeability. A separate 5,000-trial iid-normal row-count-shift challenge measured 94.56% matched-panel coverage (Wilson95 [0.939, 0.952]), 89.78% with two future observations per setting, and 81.94% with four; the exact panel score can under-cover when the future observation structure differs from calibration. D02 checks each calibration unit against the declared observations per setting and reports held-out structure mismatches; a mismatch in the independent evaluation partition is transfer counterevidence. It cannot verify an unobserved future sampling plan. An optional third independent evaluation partition reports exact-setting unit-level violations and row-count compatibility with the declared calibration panel only as descriptive diagnostics, and counts an observation only when every modeled non-explored factor matches the declared fixed setting; population-mismatched units are reported separately. When every selected candidate has exact outcomes for a common set of evaluation units, D02 also reports the fraction of those units infeasible at one or more selected cells. When up to five `evaluation_strata` fields are declared, an additional partition-composition diagnostic reports per-independent-unit category counts and categorical total-variation distances for training, calibration, and evaluation, and flags evaluation strata absent from calibration. This is descriptive counterevidence only: neither a zero distance nor matching categories establishes exchangeability, and novel evaluation strata are not covered by the calibration assumption. An absent evaluation partition or one without assignable independent units is labeled `NOT_PROVIDED` or `INSUFFICIENT_ASSIGNABLE_UNITS` and uses null unseen-stratum counts; zero is reserved for an observed evaluation partition with no unseen categories. These holdout diagnostics do not change model fitting or calibration radii and never select a favorable subset. A calibration-band miss, declared evaluation stratum absent from calibration, or exact-panel structure mismatch can only withdraw the entire candidate set; the pre-veto grid remains available for audit. A known-stratum composition difference remains a descriptive warning because empirical mixture differences alone do not establish harmful outcome drift. Missing-outcome units are excluded and reported. Cross-classified held-out challenges show tool and period shifts separately, while an unmodeled nonlinear interaction can make a searched interpolated cell falsely feasible despite narrow pointwise calibration intervals. Candidates remain advisory and require separate engineer-reviewed confirmation.

**D03 cross-step tradeoffs:** require approved temporal links to downstream outcomes; otherwise return a scope-limited within-step result. Declared objective constraints are applied at the whole independent-unit grain before aggregation, and per-setting excluded/eligible counts are surfaced. If exclusions occur, D03 withholds the family-wise envelope because outcome-dependent screening can change compared populations. Within-step comparisons require identical typed independent-unit sets at each included setting; cross-step comparisons rely on the same declared population ID in the approved synthetic contract. Cross-step marginal Student-t intervals are withheld when any setting has zero observed variance: identical observed values do not establish zero population uncertainty, and interval-separated dominance is not assessed for that metric. A bounded family-wise envelope may still be available when its separate hard-bound and precommit contract is supplied. The default family-wise envelope uses separate Bonferroni rectangles across candidate-setting × objective means with a conservative Cornish-Fisher Student-t critical approximation, and assumes approximately normal setting means, independent units, and a fixed family. It is not distribution-free. The 500-trial normal and t(5) oracle produced 95.8% and 96.8% joint coverage; a separate 500-trial skewed bounded Beta challenge showed 91.8% at 30 lots (Wilson95 [0.891, 0.939]) and 96.0% at 60 lots (Wilson95 [0.939, 0.974]), so the t envelope must not be described as shape-robust.

When a caller supplies a defensible support contract, D03 also offers a distribution-free Hoeffding/Bonferroni rectangle. The contract must declare a unique exact candidate-setting ID family, the objective metric units, metric-specific hard bounds, and source and precommit references. P4 checks that IDs and units match the analysis and that observed independent-unit aggregates are inside the supplied limits; it does not authenticate the references or infer bounds from observed extrema. The finite-sample statement is conditional on independent sampling units, truthful hard bounds for each independent-unit aggregate, and the full candidate/objective family being fixed before outcomes are examined. Any invalid or out-of-range contract disables the envelope without falling back to the t method. A seeded 500-trial challenge with four equal-mean settings, correlated repeated lots and skewed bounded Beta responses had joint coverage 100% at both 30 and 60 units (Wilson95 lower bound 0.992); mean interval widths were about 3.0–3.6 times the approximate t intervals. These finite results validate this implementation on that generator only. Wide intervals may leave most settings nondominated. Interval-separated dominance remains a diagnostic, not a causal effect or population-compatibility check; the mean Pareto front is descriptive. Never secretly choose engineering value weights or override a constraint.

**Level 3 INTERVENTION-VALIDATED:** evaluate documented experiments or interventions. Randomized comparison if truly randomized; panel DiD requires a timezone-aware intervention cutoff and source timestamp for every observation. Preperiods must precede the cutoff, post must be at/after it, and timestamps must strictly follow period order within each unit; missing or inconsistent timing is inconclusive before estimation. DiD also requires complete pre/post outcomes, assignment ledger, declared categorical `population_fields` with stable within-unit values and matching treatment/control counts, two preperiods for a pretrend placebo check, a differential-trend sensitivity bound with source, method, and units exactly matching the outcome, adequate independent units, no differential concurrent intervention, and an interval that remains directional after expanding by the declared bound. Declared concurrent-intervention IDs must be present at every observed period (`none` explicitly means none); missing labels are unknown and withhold the numerical assumption gate. D05 compares their empirical categorical proportions by arm at every available period and for every adjacent transition, so equal label sets do not hide frequency imbalance. These are diagnostics of the supplied labels, not authenticated intervention semantics or proof against confounding. Missing population fields, composition shifts or incomplete categorical coverage leave the DiD result descriptive. When a third preperiod is supplied, adjacent preperiod slope changes test for curvature; incomplete coverage or a result outside the documented equivalence bound withholds the diagnostic gates. Optional negative-control outcomes require a declared metric and matching bound units, source/method and complete compatible panels. Passing these tests cannot prove parallel trends or remove unmeasured confounding. The numeric sensitivity interval is conditional on the supplied bound; P4 cannot authenticate its source or adequacy. D05 therefore never emits `causal_claim=true` for observational DiD, and `conditional_interpretation_eligible` remains false because the available adapter has no independent substantive qualification for the bound. It reports whether the numerical assumption gates passed separately and retains the descriptive estimate and counterevidence. Statistical evidence remains separate from production qualification. Record null/harmful experiments, not only wins.

**D03 identity and objective handling:** independent-unit and join IDs are opaque strings or integers with type-preserving identity; coercing both to display strings can merge distinct engineering units and alter a mean Pareto front. A missing/invalid observed-unit ID withholds the entire comparison rather than silently dropping a row. The mean front is descriptive; marginal or family-wise uncertainty status is separately exposed, and missing or incompatible objectives prevent a cross-step comparison.

Cross-step row lineage must be an explicit array of exact source-ID strings; a scalar string that merely contains both IDs is not evidence of two-source provenance. The source registry, objective family and direction-to-metric contract are validated before any linked comparison. Empty paired inputs, duplicate objectives, conflicting directions and malformed source registries withhold the downstream claim. These local guards validate structure only: they do not authenticate company authority or the substantive relationship.

**D04 identity handling:** sequential development, grouped folds, untouched evaluation, repeated-measurement checks, incumbent means, and nearby-support counts all use the same type-preserving string/integer unit identity. Numeric ID `1` and string ID `"1"` remain distinct through every split and aggregation; missing or composite IDs are refused. This avoids row-to-unit leakage and false changes in the independent-unit count. D04's split is deterministic but caller seed and source provenance remain unauthenticated.

## Synthetic D02 independent-evaluation example

The full-workflow fixture fixes the D02 target to synthetic chamber A and uses disjoint lot partitions: 30 training, 20 calibration, and 10 independent evaluation lots in that selected chamber. Chamber B observations do not enter that fixed-population D02 fit or evaluation. Three of the 76 supported grid candidates have exact evaluation settings, so the complete-set failure audit is correctly withheld. At one observed setting, 0 of 10 evaluation lots were observed infeasible; its descriptive 95% Wilson interval is `[0, 0.2775]`. This small synthetic diagnostic is not a coverage guarantee, does not validate the other 73 candidates, and does not establish suitability for a manufacturing population.

### Exact-panel calibration transfer diagnostics

When a separate evaluation partition is supplied, P4 computes a post-selection description of whether its unit-level categorical tool/time strata and exact-setting observation counts match the caller's precommitted calibration panel. An evaluation stratum absent from calibration or a row-count/panel mismatch is recorded as counterevidence to transfer. A nonzero empirical total-variation distance among declared known strata is recorded as a descriptive composition difference. A zero distance is reported as `DESCRIPTIVE_MATCH_NO_TRANSFER_VALIDATION`; it does not validate exchangeability or future-population coverage. With no declared strata, transfer by population is `NOT_ASSESSED_NO_STRATA_DECLARED`.

The finite-panel calibration record also includes the exact active metric min/max bounds for every constrained metric. The engine canonicalizes and hashes those bounds with the metric family, panel and replicate count, and refuses the simultaneous band if the declared bounds are missing, malformed, or differ from the limits used for candidate classification. This binds the displayed threshold values to the audit record; caller-provided references and claim timing remain unauthenticated, and pointwise searched-grid candidates remain advisory.

These diagnostics are calculated after model fitting, finite-panel calibration, and candidate screening; evaluation outcomes never alter model coefficients or calibration radii. A calibration-band miss, unseen declared evaluation stratum, or exact-panel structure mismatch withholds the complete candidate set without retaining a favorable subset; the original screen remains auditable. A fixed 1,000-trial independent generator with lot-correlated errors and stratum-specific residual scales produced 94.8% coverage under the 90:10 calibration mix, 56.4% under a 10:90 shift between the same known tool strata, and 23.0% when 90% of evaluation units came from a new tool-period stratum. The experiment is a counterexample to naïve transfer under distribution shift, not a prediction of process coverage. The benchmark also records that finite samples can omit a low-frequency calibration stratum and then expose apparent unseen support in an otherwise same-mixture evaluation.

The exact P4 candidate rule was independently challenged with a held-out nonlinear interaction that is zero at every training/calibration setting and infeasible at interpolated settings. Among 25 pointwise-screened cells, 2 failed in exact-setting evaluation, and all 20 complete evaluation lots had at least one failure in the screened set. The engine withholds the entire candidate set when any exact screened setting is infeasible in independent evaluation, or when the evaluation reveals a calibration-band miss, a declared stratum absent from calibration, or an exact-panel structure mismatch; it never retains only favorable cells or asserts transfer beyond the observed evidence. The raw grid remains visible with a withheld status for audit. This is a deliberately conservative response to observed counterevidence, not a future-population estimate or general guarantee.

The exact-panel transfer workflow also scores untouched outcomes against the frozen calibration band, so a matching declared population and panel do not conceal outcome-level counterevidence. In an independent paired 250-trial challenge, each calibration/evaluation pair used the same declared `TOOL-A`/2026 mix and all nine exact settings. Baseline held-out trial miss fraction averaged 4.92%; a hidden chamber-specific nonlinear response shift of amplitude 0.025 increased it to 25.73%, and amplitude 0.05 to 88.22%; amplitudes 0.10 and 0.19 produced 100% misses in this generator. The strongest arm had counterevidence in 250/250 trials, and the paired miss fraction exceeded baseline in 250/250 trials. Trials, not pooled units, are the Monte Carlo comparison units. This demonstrates sensitivity to the specified generator only; zero misses do not validate transfer and miss fractions are descriptive, post-selection counterevidence rather than conditional or future coverage estimates.

**D04 confirmation variance:** the reported paired-difference SD is the planning input. D04 now refuses an unqualified point estimate and requires either a one-sided normal-theory chi-square upper confidence bound (at least 95%, with source degrees of freedom) or an engineer-approved hard bound. P4 records but does not recompute or authenticate that upper bound. Under a fixed normal historical-sample stress test, using a noisy point estimate as known SD led to underpowered plans in 41.1–55.8% of trials across 5–100 historical degrees of freedom; a caller-supplied one-sided 95% upper bound reduced the observed fraction to 3.0–5.15%, while increasing mean run pairs from about 35 to 43.6–141.2. These are simulation results under normal independent paired differences, not universal guarantees or engineering population evidence.

**D05 randomized missing outcomes:** assignment-ledger and observation unit IDs must be nonempty strings and match exactly; numeric `1` must never silently match string `"1"`. The ledger establishes assigned denominators. If any outcomes are missing, D05 withholds a point estimate and returns worst-case ITT arm/effect bounds only when finite caller bounds are supplied and every observed outcome lies inside them. Otherwise status is `INCONCLUSIVE`. Even a bounded result is not a point effect or verified engineering conclusion; P4 cannot authenticate randomization records or outcome bounds.

**OSS candidates:** statsmodels (baseline), DoWhy for explicit causal graphs/estimands, OR-Tools/Emukit/Ax/BoTorch for constrained designs only after an independent performance/complexity tournament, MAPIE/PyMC/pymoo for uncertainty and multiobjective scenarios as justified. Heavy dependencies are conditional, never mandatory merely from name recognition.


## Minimum correct implementation order
A. Validate source/identity/units/permissions BEFORE scientific analysis. B. Implement an independently understandable deterministic baseline and a fixed positive/negative oracle. C. Create an evidence envelope and show a complete user workflow. D. Add the full required scientific methods with assumption diagnostics and falsifying cases. E. Compare advanced OSS only where it can beat or materially extend that baseline; freeze test cases first. F. Run the combined product and real target-OS/offline qualification. G. Repeat audit until all gates pass.

## Mathematical and evaluation requirements
- For numeric estimates, specify the estimand, unit, effective independent sample, uncertainty method, algorithm/version and declared applicability domain.
- For classification, retrieval and anomaly cases, precommit held-out metrics, precision/recall/calibration or appropriately justified equivalents. Never fit thresholds to evaluation answers.
- Use group/time/device/wafer/lot/experiment-aware holdouts where dependencies require them; random measurement-row splits are not acceptable evidence of broader generalization.
- Distinguish data observed, data estimated and data unknown; refuse if physical constraints or study assumptions cannot be verified.
- Run property, metamorphic, adversarial and counterfactual *tests* where meaningful (e.g. changing labels must not create a spurious causal claim; adding an unapproved join must trigger refusal).
- An LLM explanation must be mechanically checked against the verified evidence object. An independent code-based oracle, not an agent's agreement, determines scientific pass/fail.

## Scientific peer review rubric
Engineer/reviewer should be able to answer: Is the population actually eligible? What was the measured unit and its grain? Which approved sources and joins were used? Could sampling or timing explain the observation? Is the statistical estimand identifiable? Which alternative explanations were tested? Which result is observed rather than predicted? What result would invalidate the conclusion? Does the method generalize to unseen wafers/lots/tools/periods? What is explicitly unknown?

## D02 actual-selector interpolation stress (2026-11-23)

An independent benchmark calls `explore_window` directly to measure the actual local-calibration grid selector under interpolation model misspecification. Across 250 fixed-seed trials, each with 30 training lots and 20 separate calibration lots at a 3×3 exact-setting design, the known response included a random signed odd-harmonic interaction that was exactly zero at every observed setting but nonzero between settings. P4 screened a mean of 35.636 cells per trial (34.636 interpolated); the latent mean exceeded the supplied limit at one or more selected interpolated cells in 247/250 trials (98.8%, Wilson95 [0.9653, 0.9959]). The selected-cell latent-mean violation fraction was 23.96% for interpolated cells. A predeclared exact center setting, whose true mean was feasible by construction, had zero false-feasible findings in 250 trials. The independent oracle was not supplied to P4 and no evaluation partition was passed to the selector.

This deliberately difficult held-out model-form challenge is not a field failure-rate or coverage estimate. It shows that local lot calibration at sparse exact settings cannot validate nonlinear behavior between those settings. Accordingly, D02 labels the set `MODEL_BASED_ADVISORY_SCREENING_ONLY`, reports observed/interpolated candidate counts separately, requires an explicit exact-setting independent-lot confirmation action for each screened interpolation, and limits the evidence claim to model-screened hypotheses. The deterministic read-only workflow still does not set recipes or equipment.

## D04 chronological validation for sequential experiments

`experiment_design.validation_time_split` can define `timestamp_field`, timezone-aware `training_end` and `evaluation_start`, and a caller-supplied `split_reference`. Every independent unit must stay wholly on one side; missing timestamps, boundary-crossing units and rows in the gap are refused rather than dropped. Surface choice and model fitting use development units; the later holdout is only an engineer-specified RMSE gate and remains unauthenticated and descriptive. A fixed 100-trial synthetic quadratic challenge with a +0.5 late-lot shift and a 0.2 RMSE limit had random-group proposals pass in 74/100 trials (Wilson95 64.6–81.6%) while chronological proposals passed in 0/100 (upper Wilson95 3.7%). This shows the random split can mix drift into both partitions; it does not establish future or manufacturing performance. The panel displays split type, boundaries, independent-unit counts, selected formula, RMSE, and caller qualification status.
